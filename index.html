About

Seungwon Yang is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH. His research focuses on building human-centered systems that support social interaction and human agency, primarily through AR/VR applications.

He has attended CHI 2024, CHI 2025, and UIST 2025. He envisions a future where technology empowers people in their everyday lives by supporting their agency, confidence, and meaningful social connections.

He is also interested in how the AI-driven democratization of software creation reshapes the relationship between people and technology.

He received his bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from POSTECH in 2023.
